# Review Session

1. What is CTI?
    - Gathering Information to make decisions understand the landscape
    - Threat Hunting
    - To find Potential Threats

2. What are the Process for CTI?
    - Gather information Data
    - Analyze it...
        - Define Patterns
        - Normal and abnormal behavior/ - Threats
    - Looking for IOCs, Vulnerabilities
    - Disseminate / Report/ Document

3. How do we find IOCs?
    - Historical Data - MITRE ATT&CK
    - Threat Intelligence - 
        - OSINT, HMNINT, SIGINT, TECHINT

4. What are TTPs?
    - Tactic Technique and Procedures
    - Vulnerabilities, exploits from APTs

5. What Are some IOCs?
    - FileNames, File Hashes, IPs,Domain Names, URLs, Process Names, Registry, Email addresses, Traffic Pattern

6. What are the three levels of CTI?
    1. Strategic - Business Decision makers, Executives - making decisions, changing strategies, Roadmap, big picture, policies/governance

    2. Operational - Daily Operations - Priorities, Resource Allocation - IOCs, Tasks

    3. Tactical - Technical, specific - IOCs, Vulnerabilites, exploits, TTPs, treat as it currently happens
